Oikos is the New Testament word for household — the people God has already placed around you. Write your name in the centre, then add names in each circle: people you see regularly who do not yet follow Jesus, or who need encouragement. Aim for at least five.

1 · PrayPray for each name, every day, for an open door.
2 · CareShow up, serve a need, make space to listen.
3 · ShareTell your story and the good news when a door opens.
4 · DiscipleRead Scripture together and help them reach their oikos.

Pray (2 Corinthians 4:4)

Ask God to open their eyes to the light of the Gospel, soften their heart, and send someone to them — maybe you.

Care (Matthew 5:16)

A meal, a message, help with a move, an honest "how are you really?" Love that shows up opens doors.

Share (1 Peter 3:15)

Your story in three minutes: life before Jesus, how you met Him, what is different now. Then invite them.

Disciple (2 Timothy 2:2)

Read a Gospel story together each week. Ask: what does this show about God? About people? What will I do?
